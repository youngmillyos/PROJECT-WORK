Content Writing

WordCraft Studio

Location: Nigeria, Edo State

Salary: ₦5,000 - ₦25,000

Type: Part-time / Freelance

Job Description

We are seeking a creative and detail-oriented content writer to create engaging blog posts, social media captions, website content, and promotional copy. The ideal candidate should be able to research topics, write clearly, and tailor content to different audiences while maintaining strong grammar and originality.

Requirements

  • Excellent writing, editing, and proofreading skills
  • Ability to research and write on different topics
  • Strong understanding of grammar, tone, and audience targeting
  • Ability to meet deadlines and work on multiple assignments
